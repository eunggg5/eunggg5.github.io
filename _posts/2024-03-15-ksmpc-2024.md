The **67th Conference of the Korean Society for Music Perception and Cognition (KSMPC)** was held in Seoul.

I presented: *"Emotion inference during music and social situations through empathic accuracy."*

Our lab also presented several other works:
- *Correlations between music experience and EEG responses* (Park et al.)
- *Evaluation of AI-generated Music through Online Surveys* (Kim et al.)
