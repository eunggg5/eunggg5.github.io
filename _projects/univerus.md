**KAIST × Daejeon Arts Center [X-Space] #2 — UNIVER:US**
*September 28, 2024*

This performance explores how musical experiences manifest in brain activity. Using real-time brainwave processing and synchronization algorithms, the project visualizes neural responses during musical scenarios.

The piece demonstrates the phenomenon of **brainwave synchronization** — when people share emotional musical experiences, their neural patterns align with one another. When we become emotionally connected through music, our brainwaves also align.

**Collaborators:** KAIST Music and Brain Lab, Daejeon Arts Center
