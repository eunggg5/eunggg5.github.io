The **17th International Conference on Music Perception and Cognition (ICMPC)** was held in Tokyo, Japan.

I presented:
- *Musical pleasure revealed by electroencephalography theta oscillations: A systematic review*
- *Effect of online music class on linguistic, cognitive, and musical abilities in Korean preschool children: A pilot study*

Our lab also presented a comparison of melody interpolation by human and AI (Kim et al.).
